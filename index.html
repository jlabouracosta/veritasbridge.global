The evidence gap

Funding decisions rest on less evidence than the stakes deserve.

Funders making decisions about organizations in Latin America and the Caribbean have long relied on relationships, references, and reputation. That knowledge is real — but it's uneven, hard to verify, and rarely written down. Independent, documented evidence of what an organization has actually built has always been the scarce part.

The recent drawdown of U.S. development funding, including USAID, has thinned the shared reference points many funders once leaned on, leaving more of that judgment to each funder with less to go on. The need for independent evidence isn't new — it's simply harder to meet now.

Veritas Bridge Partners provides that evidence: an independent, documented account of where an organization stands — so a decision can rest on more than reputation.

For organizations

Whether a funder has asked you to take part, or you're exploring an assessment on your own.

A BRIDGE Profile™ documents your organization's standing through independent, verified evidence — a clear, credible account of what you've built, in a form funders recognize.

  • It is not an audit, a grade, or a pass/fail test, and it does not decide whether anyone funds you.
  • Your evidence counts in whatever form it lives — a handwritten log, a community assembly's minutes, a message thread where a real decision was made.
  • You are considered in light of what you are. A small, community-rooted organization is never held to the paperwork of a large, well-funded one.
  • The results are yours. You decide whether, when, and with whom to share them.

Who we are

Over two decades of institutional philanthropy, turned into evidence.

Veritas Bridge Partners was founded by Joaquin A. Labour-Acosta, an information systems and data intelligence expert whose career spans both technology and institutional philanthropy. Beginning with the design and implementation of information systems, he later applied data-driven decision frameworks, compliance oversight, and due diligence practices within institutions responsible for deploying philanthropic capital across multiple regions of the world, including Rockefeller Philanthropy Advisors, one of the world's largest philanthropic service organizations.

Over nearly two decades working at the intersection of grantmaking operations, risk management, compliance, and organizational review, he recognized a persistent challenge: funders are often required to make consequential investment decisions without access to the level of independent evidence those decisions warrant. Veritas Bridge Partners was established to bridge that gap, providing rigorous, objective assessments that strengthen confidence, accountability, and impact in philanthropic decision-making.
