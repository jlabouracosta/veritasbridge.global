Strong funding relationships in Latin America and the Caribbean depend on something that is too often missing: a shared, trusted basis of evidence. Veritas Bridge Partners provides it. Why now

The institutional landscape has changed. The need for evidence has not.

Funders placing capital across Latin America and the Caribbean have always faced a hard question: how do you know, independently, whether an organization is what its paperwork says it is? For years, that question was easier to answer than it looks — a broader layer of field presence, monitoring, and shared institutional knowledge made unfamiliar organizations easier to understand from a distance. It was never a complete picture, but it was a common reference.

That reference has grown thinner. The restructuring of U.S. foreign assistance, including the dismantling of USAID, fragmented a significant part of that infrastructure — and the burden of understanding an organization now falls more heavily on each funder, often without the field presence to carry it. The consequence is not abstract: capital is committed, and intended for the ground, with far less independent evidence behind it than the stakes deserve.

The practical question is the one that remains: who independently verifies what the available documentation actually shows? That is the gap Veritas Bridge Partners was built to close.

When institutional infrastructure changes, independent evidence matters more.

What's at stake

Every funding decision carries two risks.

False positive

Funding the wrong organization

Committing capital to an organization whose apparent capacity, governance, or controls do not hold up to closer, independent examination.

False negative

Overlooking the right one

Passing over a capable, community-rooted organization because its evidence of strength does not fit the shape of conventional due diligence.

A BRIDGE Profile™ helps funders reduce both — through independent evidence, verification, and regional context, so consequential decisions are made with greater confidence.

For funders

Whether evaluating a potential grantee or strengthening your due diligence process in a complex funding environment.


A BRIDGE Profile™ provides independent, evidence-based documentation of an organization's governance, operations, financial stewardship, and demonstrated capacity — designed to supplement, not replace, your existing grantmaking, compliance, and decision-making processes.

  • It strengthens your due diligence. Independently verified evidence and regional context that reinforce your own assessments and decision-making frameworks.
  • It reads organizations in context. Each is considered relative to its operating environment, resources, and stage of development — the realities of working across Latin America and the Caribbean.
  • The decision stays yours. How the evidence informs funding, compliance, or risk decisions is entirely up to you.

Independent evidence, not a funding verdict. We do not recommend whom to fund, raise or administer funds, act as an intermediary, or substitute for legal, audit, or compliance counsel.

For organizations

Whether a funder has asked you to take part, or you're exploring an assessment on your own.

A BRIDGE Profile™ documents your organization's standing through independent, verified evidence — a clear, credible account of what you've built, in a form funders recognize.

  • It is not an audit, a grade, or a pass/fail test, and it does not decide whether anyone funds you.
  • Your evidence counts in whatever form it lives — a handwritten log, a community assembly's minutes, a message thread where a real decision was made.
  • You are considered in light of what you are. A small, community-rooted organization is never held to the paperwork of a large, well-funded one.
  • The results are yours. You decide whether, when, and with whom to share them.

Why we exist

Who stands behind the work

Veritas Bridge Partners was founded by Joaquín A. Labour-Acosta, an information systems and data intelligence expert whose career spans both technology and institutional philanthropy. He has spent nearly two decades applying data-driven decision frameworks, compliance oversight, and due-diligence practices within major institutions responsible for deploying philanthropic capital — including managing due diligence and compliance across a $270 million national and international grantmaking portfolio at Rockefeller Philanthropy Advisors, and holding grants and operations roles at the Prudential Foundation and the F.B. Heron Foundation. A graduate of the Universidad Nacional Pedro Henríquez Ureña in the Dominican Republic and bilingual in English and Spanish, he built Veritas Bridge Partners to bring the discipline of institutional due diligence to a region where a lack of formal documentation is too often mistaken for a lack of capability.

Veritas Bridge Partners is built on a methodology he developed from years of assessing organizations from the inside: The BRIDGE Profile™, an evidence-based reading of an organization's standing across seven dimensions — The Seven Pillars of Organizational Standing™. Rather than reduce an organization to a single score, it documents where an organization genuinely stands — from how its mission drives its decisions, to the strength of its financial stewardship, to the outcomes it can demonstrate — each rated against independently verified evidence. It is the framework that turns the diligence gap into something a funder can actually see.

Every review is led personally by the founder, drawing on specialist expertise in finance, organizational capacity, and local context as each engagement requires — so the depth of the work never rests on a single perspective.
